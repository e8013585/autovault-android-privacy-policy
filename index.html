AutoVault Privacy Policy

Effective October 25, 2026 · Developer: Unpossible Apps

AutoVault is an offline vehicle maintenance logbook with service reminders, fuel tracking, and estimates and invoices for workshops. It has no account, no cloud service and no servers of its own. This policy explains what the app does and doesn't do with information.

Your data stays on your device

Your vehicles, odometer readings, maintenance items, service records, diagnostics, fuel log, photos, customers, estimates, invoices, shop profile and settings are stored only in the app's private storage on your device. We don't operate any servers for AutoVault, so we can't see, access, back up or recover your data, and we never receive it. Reminders and cost calculations run entirely on your device.

Customer information (workshop mode)

If you use AutoVault for a business, you may enter details about your customers, such as names, phone numbers, email and postal addresses, and their vehicles. This information stays on your device, is used only to fill in your estimates and invoices, and is never sent to us. You are responsible for handling your customers' information in line with the laws that apply to your business.

Information processed by Google services in the app

  • Google Play Billing processes purchases of AutoVault Pro subscriptions (monthly or yearly). Payments are handled entirely by Google Play; we never receive your payment details. The app checks with Google Play on your device to confirm whether your subscription is active.
  • Advertising. Version 1.0 shows no ads, and the advertising SDK included in the app is never started. A future version may show ads to free users through Google AdMob. If it does, this policy will be updated before that version is released, you'll be asked for consent where the law requires it, and AutoVault Pro will remain ad-free.

Apart from Google Play Billing, the app makes no network connections.

Permissions

  • Camera (optional) is used only when you choose to photograph a receipt or part. Photos are saved in the app's private storage. You can also pick existing photos with Android's photo picker, which needs no permission and shares only the photos you select.
  • Notifications (optional) remind you when maintenance is due soon or overdue, and when it's time to log the odometer.
  • Network access is used only by Google Play Billing (and, if ever enabled, the advertising SDK described above).
  • AutoVault doesn't access your contacts, location, microphone or other files. Files you export (PDFs and backups) are written only where you choose, and files you restore are opened only when you pick them.

Things we don't do

  • No account or sign-in, and no analytics, crash reporting or tracking.
  • We don't sell, rent or share your personal information or your customers' information.
  • We don't build a profile of you or your vehicles.

Exports, sharing and backups

Android's automatic cloud backup is turned off for AutoVault. When you save or share an estimate or invoice as a PDF, or create a backup file, the file goes only where you choose: a folder on your device, a cloud drive you pick, or an app you share it with. Those destinations are governed by their own privacy policies. Backup files contain your app data and photos and are not encrypted, so store them somewhere you trust. We never receive these files.

Deleting your data

You can delete any vehicle, record, photo, customer or document in the app; deleting a vehicle also deletes its history and photos. Uninstalling the app permanently removes all of its data from your device. To end an AutoVault Pro subscription, cancel it in Google Play under Payments & subscriptions.

Children

AutoVault isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children.
